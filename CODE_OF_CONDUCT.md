# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the acurl
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@tragent.io**. All reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code.
